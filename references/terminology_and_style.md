## Language Guard

Before final prose, check:

- Use preferred technical terms consistently.
- Replace vague terms such as "good", "bad", "obvious", "very", "huge", and
  "breakthrough".
- Downgrade causal verbs when evidence is associative.
- Match field-standard units, endpoint names, and assay names.
- Keep claims at the strength allowed by the Evidence Ledger.
- Avoid overusing generic AI-like transitions.

## Safe Style Adaptation

Allowed:

- Adopt field-specific terminology.
- Use common academic phrase patterns.
- Match section-level tone and claim strength.
- Learn how limitations and implications are framed.

Not allowed:

- Copy distinctive sentence structure or rare phrasing.
- Reproduce paragraphs from source papers.
- Replace scientific precision with journal-like rhetoric.
