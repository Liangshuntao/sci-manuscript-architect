# Reviewer Audit

Use this before finalizing a section or manuscript. The audit should be direct
and risk-focused, not polite editing feedback.

## Reviewer Lenses

Check the manuscript through these reviewer perspectives:

- Novelty reviewer: Is the contribution new enough and scoped honestly?
- Methods reviewer: Can the work be reproduced from the Methods?
- Statistics reviewer: Are tests, sample sizes, effect sizes, and uncertainty
  reported appropriately?
- Evidence reviewer: Are claims supported by figures, tables, citations, and
  user data?
- Clinical reviewer: Are translational claims justified by the population,
  model, and outcomes?
- Journal editor: Does the paper fit the target journal's article type,
  structure, and interest?

## Required Output

| Risk ID | Location | Reviewer Concern | Severity | Evidence Basis | Required Fix | Suggested English Revision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Severity:

- `critical`: likely rejection or invalid conclusion.
- `major`: must fix before submission.
- `moderate`: likely reviewer comment.
- `minor`: clarity or presentation issue.

## Overclaiming Checks

Flag:

- Causal claims from observational or associative evidence.
- Mechanism claims without perturbation, pathway, or validation data.
- Clinical utility claims from non-clinical or exploratory data.
- Superiority claims without direct comparator and statistics.
- Broad population claims from narrow samples.
- "First", "novel", or "breakthrough" without literature support.
