# Submission-Level Estimator

## Purpose

Use this reference to estimate realistic submission tier and upgrade paths.
This is strategic guidance, not an acceptance prediction.

## Submission Tiers

- **Top/high-impact**: strong field motivation, clear novelty, multi-layer or
  clinically meaningful evidence, strong validation, and broad relevance.
- **Solid specialty**: clear question, reliable data, field-relevant novelty,
  and adequate validation for a focused audience.
- **Method/resource**: reusable dataset, atlas, tool, benchmark, workflow, or
  computational method with quality control and reproducibility.
- **Descriptive/lower-level**: mostly descriptive, small sample, weak novelty,
  limited validation, or unclear clinical/mechanistic advance.

## Required Inputs

- Field Scan Report or `FIELD_SCAN_REQUIRED`.
- Motivation and Gap Lock.
- Innovation and Contribution Matrix.
- Evidence Ledger.
- Figure Architecture Plan when available.
- Target journal or target tier if supplied by the user.

## Estimation Logic

Assess:

- Field heat: crowded, emerging, niche, or underdeveloped.
- Novelty: already known, incremental, solid, or field-shaping.
- Evidence strength: descriptive, associative, validated, causal, clinical.
- Translational value: none, plausible, target/biomarker relevant, guideline
  relevant, clinical-decision relevant.
- Journal fit: audience, article type, methodological expectations, validation
  requirements, word/figure limits.

## Output

Always include:

- Recommended tier.
- Rationale by evidence axis.
- Main rejection risks.
- Upgrade path to the next tier.
- Risk labels: `FIELD_SCAN_REQUIRED`, `EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`,
  `VALIDATION_REQUIRED`, `CLAIM_OVERREACH_RISK`.
