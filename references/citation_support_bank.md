# Citation Support Bank

Use this bank to connect literature to specific manuscript claims. It is a
candidate pool, not a bibliography dump.

## Required Table

| Candidate ID | Reference or BibTeX | Year | Recency | Supports Section | Support Claim Sentence | Why This Paper Fits | Source | Verified | Verification Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Pair each paper with one or two sentences it can legitimately support.
- Mark unverified entries as `VERIFY`; do not use them in final prose as
  confirmed references.
- Prefer recent literature for active biomedical fields. In 2026, treat 2023 or
  later as recent unless the field moves slowly.
- Include a balanced mix of foundational, same-field, adjacent-field, review,
  method, and application references.
- Do not cite exemplar papers for their rhetorical structure unless they also
  support a scientific claim.
- Do not invent DOI, PMID, journal, year, author, or title details.

## Final Citation Selection

Before drafting:

- Select only the citations that support actual manuscript claims.
- Avoid inserting every candidate.
- Check that Introduction and Discussion citations serve different functions:
  Introduction establishes gap and rationale; Discussion interprets findings,
  limits, and implications.
