# Field Positioning

## Purpose

Use this reference before manuscript drafting when the user asks about field
progress, novelty, contribution level, or submission strategy. The goal is to
place the project in an external biomedical field coordinate system rather than
judging only from internal data.

## Core Questions

- What is the disease, biological process, cell type, pathway, assay, cohort,
  or intervention space?
- What has changed in the last 3-5 years?
- Are recent papers mostly discovery, mechanism, clinical, methods, resource,
  or review papers?
- Are there guidelines, consensus statements, or translational signals showing
  real clinical need?
- Are the target, pathway, biomarker, or mechanism mature, understudied, or
  already clinically saturated?

## ToolUniverse Sources

Use only the needed tools:

- `PubMed_search_articles`: recent field papers, reviews, major journals,
  publication-year trend.
- `PMC_search_papers`: open full-text examples for deeper reading.
- `iCite_search_publications`: citation count, RCR, APT, NIH percentile,
  human/animal/molecular flags.
- `MeSH_search_descriptors`: normalize disease, intervention, anatomy, and
  concept terms.
- `BioPortal_annotate_text`: annotate unclear project summaries or abstracts.
- `PubMed_Guidelines_Search` and `TRIP_Database_Guidelines_Search`: clinical
  guidelines and unmet need.
- `Pharos_search_targets` and `TargetMine_search`: target maturity,
  druggability, target-disease associations, and translational relevance.

## Field Scan Output

Report:

- Field definition and normalized terms.
- Search scope, date range, and databases used.
- Recent field movement: growing, stable, crowded, declining, or niche.
- Anchor papers and review papers.
- Guideline or clinical unmet-need signals.
- Target/pathway maturity if relevant.
- What the project must prove to be publishable at each journal tier.

If no live scan has been performed, mark the section `FIELD_SCAN_REQUIRED` and
state that novelty and submission-level judgments are provisional.
